# MySql-Major-Employee-Management-System-
Employee Management System using SQL to manage employee records, departments, payroll, qualifications, and leave data efficiently. Designed with ER diagrams, relational database concepts, and analytical SQL queries to generate business insights and improve HR operations.

# Employee Management System

## Project Overview

The **Employee Management System** is a SQL-based database project designed to efficiently manage employee-related information within an organization.
The system centralizes employee records such as departments, job roles, salaries, payroll, qualifications, and leave details in a structured relational database.

This project demonstrates the use of **SQL queries, ER modeling, relational database concepts, and data analysis techniques** to generate meaningful business insights and improve HR operations.

---

#  Objectives

* Manage employee information in a centralized database
* Track departments, job roles, and salary details
* Maintain employee qualification and leave records
* Simplify payroll management processes

# ER Diagram

The ER Diagram represents the relationship between different tables in the Employee Management System.

### Key Relationships

* Each employee belongs to a department
* Employees have salary and payroll records
* Employees can possess qualifications
* Leave records are connected to employees
* Primary and Foreign Keys maintain relational integrity

#  SQL Analysis Performed

The project includes multiple SQL queries for business analysis such as:

1. Total unique employees
2. Departments with highest employees
3. Average salary per department
4. Top 5 highest-paid employees
5. Total salary expenditure
6. Number of job roles per department
7. Average salary range per department
8. Job roles with highest salary
9. Departments with highest salary allocation
10. Employees with qualifications
11. Positions requiring qualifications
12. Employees with highest qualifications
13. Year with most leaves
14. Average leaves per department
15. Departments with highest bonuses

---

# Key Insights

* Finance and IT departments have the highest employee count
* Legal department has the highest average salary
* Total organizational salary expenditure exceeds 4.3 million
* Leave distribution remains balanced across departments
* Bonus allocation is highest in Finance and IT departments
